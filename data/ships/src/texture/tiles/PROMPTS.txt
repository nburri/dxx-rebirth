Hull tiles of the fork's own ships - prompts and provenance

Generator: Scenario (https://www.scenario.com), model "Scenario Texture"
(model_scenario-texture), text-to-image only (no image input), 1024x1024,
quality "medium", eraseSeam on, 12 compute units per picture.  Two
pictures per prompt; variant 1 of each was kept and scaled to 256x256 by
our script.  The ships' atlases are painted from these tiles by
../texture_ships.py (recoloured per ship and material, plus procedural
detail).
